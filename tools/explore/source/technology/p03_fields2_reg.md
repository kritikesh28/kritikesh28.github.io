## TEC-11 Medical technology

> **Before you start — TEC-11.** Medical technology is the long list the brief asks for. Every medical claim on the website will cite peer-reviewed reviews or regulators (WHO, CDSCO, US FDA). **This index gives information, not medical advice.**

- ☐ **TEC-11.1 Diagnostics**: stethoscope (René Laennec, 1816), thermometer, blood pressure cuff (Riva-Rocci, 1896), lab tests, rapid tests, ECG (Willem Einthoven, Nobel 1924), pulse oximeter (Takuo Aoyagi, 1970s), point-of-care devices.
- ☐ **TEC-11.2 Imaging**: X-rays (Wilhelm Röntgen, 1895; first Nobel in Physics, 1901), ultrasound, CT (Godfrey Hounsfield and Allan Cormack, Nobel 1979), MRI (Paul Lauterbur and Peter Mansfield, Nobel 2003), PET, AI-assisted reading of scans. *Note:* India's PCPNDT Act bans sex determination by ultrasound.
- ☐ **TEC-11.3 Surgery**: anaesthesia (1846), antisepsis (Joseph Lister, 1867), Sushruta's ancient descriptions of rhinoplasty, laparoscopic (keyhole) surgery, robotic surgery, lasers.
- ☐ **TEC-11.4 Prosthetics and implants**: the Jaipur Foot (P. K. Sethi and Ram Chandra Sharma, 1968), artificial joints, pacemakers (1958), cochlear implants (QEPrize 2026), stents, brain-computer interfaces.
- ☐ **TEC-11.5 Pharmaceuticals**: aspirin (1897), insulin (1921 to 1922), penicillin (Fleming, 1928; mass-produced in the 1940s); India as the "pharmacy of the world" for generic medicines.
- ☐ **TEC-11.6 Vaccines**: smallpox vaccination (Edward Jenner, 1796; variolation earlier in India, China and Africa), polio vaccines (Salk, Sabin), mRNA vaccines (Katalin Karikó and Drew Weissman, Nobel 2023); India's Covaxin and Covishield; Serum Institute of India, the world's largest vaccine maker by doses.
- ☐ **TEC-11.7 Telemedicine and digital health**: eSanjeevani (2019), the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (2021), CoWIN.
- ☐ **TEC-11.8 Medical devices for hospitals**: ventilators, dialysis machines (Willem Kolff, 1943), incubators, oxygen concentrators, radiotherapy.
- ☐ **TEC-11.9 Neurotechnology**: EEG, deep brain stimulation, optogenetics (Nobel 2026, Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel).

## TEC-12 Communication, computing and AI

- ☐ **TEC-12.1 Communication**: drums and smoke signals, postal systems (India Post, 1854), the telegraph (Samuel Morse, 1837 to 1844; India's Calcutta–Diamond Harbour line, 1851), telephone (Alexander Graham Bell, 1876; Antonio Meucci earlier), radio (Marconi; J. C. Bose's millimetre-wave work, 1895), television, satellites, the internet, mobile.
- ☐ **TEC-12.2 Computing**: the abacus, Indian decimal place value and zero (Brahmagupta, 628 CE), Charles Babbage's engines and Ada Lovelace's notes (1843), Alan Turing (1936), ENIAC (1945), the transistor, the microprocessor (Intel 4004, 1971), personal computers, the cloud.
- ☐ **TEC-12.3 Software**: operating systems (Unix, 1969; Linux, 1991), programming languages (FORTRAN, C, Python), open source, India's IT services industry (TCS, 1968; Infosys, 1981; Wipro).
- ☐ **TEC-12.4 Artificial intelligence**: the term coined at Dartmouth (1956); expert systems; machine learning; deep learning (the 2018 Turing Award to Yoshua Bengio, Geoffrey Hinton and Yann LeCun; Hinton and John Hopfield's Nobel in Physics, 2024); AlphaFold (Nobel in Chemistry 2024 to Demis Hassabis and John Jumper, with David Baker); large language models and generative AI; the IndiaAI Mission (2024).
- ☐ **TEC-12.5 Quantum computing and quantum information**: qubits, quantum cryptography (Turing Award 2025 to Charles Bennett and Gilles Brassard); India's National Quantum Mission (2023).
- ☐ **TEC-12.6 Cybersecurity**: passwords and two-factor authentication, encryption, CERT-In, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (2023).

## TEC-13 Space, defence and materials

- ☐ **TEC-13.1 Space**: Sputnik (1957), Gagarin (1961), Apollo 11 (1969); ISRO (1969) under Vikram Sarabhai; Aryabhata (1975), SLV-3 (1980), INSAT, PSLV, Chandrayaan-1 (2008, water on the Moon), Mangalyaan (2014, Mars orbit on the first attempt), Chandrayaan-3 (2023), Aditya-L1 (2024), SpaDeX docking (2025), Shubhanshu Shukla on the International Space Station (Axiom-4, 2025); Gaganyaan human spaceflight in preparation; NavIC navigation.
- ☐ **TEC-13.2 Defence technology**: from bows and *wootz* steel to gunpowder, radar, missiles; India's DRDO (1958), the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme under A. P. J. Abdul Kalam (1983), BrahMos, Agni, Tejas, Akash. Presented as history and engineering, not as weapons advice.
- ☐ **TEC-13.3 Materials**: copper, bronze and iron ages; *wootz* (crucible) steel of South India, prized as "Damascus steel"; plastics (Bakelite, 1907); semiconductors (silicon); composites, graphene (isolated 2004; Nobel 2010), superalloys; India's semiconductor mission (2021) and first chip fabs.

## TEC-14 Biotechnology, environment, finance, education, entertainment and assistive technology

- ☐ **TEC-14.1 Biotechnology**: fermentation, the DNA double helix (1953), recombinant DNA (1973), PCR (Kary Mullis, 1983), the Human Genome Project (2003), CRISPR gene editing (Jennifer Doudna and Emmanuelle Charpentier, Nobel 2020); India's Bt cotton (2002), Genome India project, Biocon.
- ☐ **TEC-14.2 Environmental technology**: pollution control, catalytic converters, air-quality monitors, waste-to-energy, recycling, carbon capture, e-waste processing; India's E-Waste (Management) Rules (2022).
- ☐ **TEC-14.3 Financial technology**: coins (India's punch-marked coins), paper money, banks, ATMs (1967; India's first, HSBC Mumbai, 1987), credit cards, online banking, UPI (NPCI, 2016; over 20 billion transactions a month by 2025), RuPay, digital rupee pilots, Aadhaar-enabled payments; risks: fraud and scams.
- ☐ **TEC-14.4 Education technology**: the blackboard, printed textbooks, radio and TV classes (SITE 1975), MOOCs, SWAYAM (2017), DIKSHA, AI tutors; the digital divide.
- ☐ **TEC-14.5 Entertainment technology**: cinema (MOV), recorded sound (MUS), radio, TV, video games (SPT), streaming, VR.
- ☐ **TEC-14.6 Assistive technology**: Braille (Louis Braille, 1824; Bharati Braille for Indian scripts), white canes, hearing aids, screen readers, wheelchairs, sign-language apps; India's Accessible India Campaign (2015) and ADIP scheme.

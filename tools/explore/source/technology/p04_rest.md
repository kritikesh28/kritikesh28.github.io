# PART 8 — IDEAS: PHILOSOPHY, PSYCHOLOGY AND SOCIETY

> **About this Part.** The philosophy and psychology angles of the brief. Ideas are presented fairly, with sources.

## TEC-20 Philosophy of technology

- ☐ **TEC-20.1 Tools as extensions of the body**: Ernst Kapp (1877) and Marshall McLuhan's *Understanding Media* (1964): "the medium is the message"; the wheel extends the foot, the book the eye.
- ☐ **TEC-20.2 Gandhi on machines**: *Hind Swaraj* (1909) criticised machinery that displaced people; later Gandhi welcomed machines that served people, such as the Singer sewing machine; the *charkha* as a symbol of self-reliance.
- ☐ **TEC-20.3 Appropriate technology**: E. F. Schumacher, *Small Is Beautiful* (1973), drew on Gandhian ideas; intermediate technology for villages.
- ☐ **TEC-20.4 Is technology neutral?**: Langdon Winner's "Do Artifacts Have Politics?" (1980); Heidegger's "The Question Concerning Technology" (1954); Lewis Mumford.
- ☐ **TEC-20.5 Technological determinism versus social shaping**: does technology drive society, or society shape technology? Most historians now say both.

## TEC-21 Technology and society

- ☐ **TEC-21.1 Technology and jobs**: the Luddites (1811 to 1816), automation, AI and work; studies differ on how many jobs will change; new skills and social protection.
- ☐ **TEC-21.2 Privacy and surveillance**: data protection, India's Supreme Court ruling that privacy is a fundamental right (*Puttaswamy*, 2017), the DPDP Act (2023).
- ☐ **TEC-21.3 E-waste and the environment**: the Global E-waste Monitor (2024) counted about 62 million tonnes of e-waste in 2022, with under a quarter documented as recycled; mining for critical minerals; energy use of data centres.
- ☐ **TEC-21.4 Repair culture and right to repair**: repair cafés; the EU's right-to-repair rules; India's Right to Repair portal (2022) under the Department of Consumer Affairs.
- ☐ **TEC-21.5 Digital well-being**: screen time, attention and sleep; WHO guidance for young children's screen time (2019); notification hygiene.
- ☐ **TEC-21.6 The digital divide and inclusion**: access by gender, region and income; Indian-language interfaces; accessibility.
- ☐ **TEC-21.7 AI ethics and safety**: bias, misinformation and deepfakes, copyright, safety research; India's IT Rules amendments on synthetic media; the EU AI Act (2024).
- ☐ **TEC-21.8 Psychology of technology**: the "uncanny valley" (Masahiro Mori, 1970), the endowment effect with gadgets, variable rewards in apps, technostress.

# PART 9 — THE PRACTICAL GUIDE

## TEC-22 Choosing, caring for and recycling devices

- ☐ **TEC-22.1 How to choose a device**: decide your needs first; compare BEE star labels, warranty, service network, repairability and software-update years; read independent reviews with test data.
- ☐ **TEC-22.2 Care**: keep vents clear, avoid heat, charge with certified chargers, update software, clean dust; descale geysers and kettles.
- ☐ **TEC-22.3 Electrical safety**: earthing, MCBs and RCCBs (residual-current breakers save lives), ISI-marked wiring, never touch switches with wet hands, unplug during storms.
- ☐ **TEC-22.4 Battery safety**: do not puncture or overheat lithium batteries; use proper chargers; dispose of them at collection points.
- ☐ **TEC-22.5 Cyber safety**: strong passwords and a password manager, two-factor authentication, never share OTPs, report fraud at cybercrime.gov.in or helpline 1930.
- ☐ **TEC-22.6 Right to repair and recycling**: use authorised e-waste collectors (CPCB list); donate working devices; remove personal data before giving away.
- ☐ **TEC-22.7 Learning technology**: maker spaces, Atal Tinkering Labs in schools (2016), online courses, electronics kits, coding clubs.
- ☐ **TEC-22.8 Careers**: engineering branches, ITI trades, diploma courses, research, design, repair entrepreneurship.

## What it is

Curious Sparoows is the website you are on: a Library that starts every subject from a small question, an Exam Prep section for RPSC and RSSB aspirants, and this portfolio. It works in English and Hindi.

## How it is built

- **Astro** turns Markdown and JSON into about 3,000 plain HTML pages, so pages load fast and need no server.
- **TypeScript** runs the small interactive parts: the menus, the question slider, the reading pages and the background chirps.
- **HTML and CSS** carry the design: light and dark themes, phone-first layouts and no tracking cookies.
- **Python** scripts turn Google Sheets and Word books into website data (the Curriculum Index, the Exam Guide, the Universal Index of Human Knowledge and the encyclopedic shelves).
- **GitHub Pages** hosts it; every change goes through a pull request and deploys on its own.

## What I learned

Structure first. Every page sits on one tree (Forest, Tree, Branch, Twig, Leaf), so a new subject only needs one line to find its place on the site.

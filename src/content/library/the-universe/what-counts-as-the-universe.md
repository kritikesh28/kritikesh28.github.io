## One word for everything

The word comes from the Latin *universum*: "everything, taken together as one". The Universe is not a place that holds things. It **is** all the things, plus the space and time they exist in.

That means you are inside it, not looking at it from outside. There is no outside that we know of.

## The levels, small to large

It is easier to picture with nested levels, like an address:

| Level | What it is | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| Planet | A large body orbiting a star | Earth |
| Planetary system | A star and everything orbiting it | The Solar System |
| Galaxy | Billions of stars, gas, dust and dark matter bound by gravity | The Milky Way |
| Group or cluster | Galaxies held together by gravity | The Local Group |
| Supercluster | Huge sheets and chains of clusters | Laniakea |
| Observable Universe | Everything whose light has reached us | |

Your cosmic address is: Earth, Solar System, Milky Way, Local Group, Laniakea Supercluster, observable Universe.

## Words people often mix up

- **Space** is the expanse between things. The **Universe** is space plus time, matter and energy.
- **Galaxy** is one star city. The **Universe** holds hundreds of billions of them, perhaps more.
- **Astronomy** studies objects such as stars and planets. **Cosmology** studies the Universe as a whole: its birth, shape and fate.

## Remember this

The Universe is the biggest "everything" there is, and Earth is one small address inside it.

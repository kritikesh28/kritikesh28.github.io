## The whole story in one breath

A hot, dense early Universe expanded and cooled. Particles formed, then nuclei, then atoms. The fog cleared and light flew free. Gravity gathered gas into the first stars and galaxies. Stars forged heavier elements, and from that recycled dust our Sun, Earth and we ourselves were made. The Universe is still expanding, faster and faster, and we still do not know exactly why.

## Five numbers worth remembering

| Number | What it is |

| 13.8 billion years | Age of the Universe |

| 4.6 billion years ago | When the Sun and Earth formed |
| 5 / 27 / 68 | Percent ordinary matter / dark matter / dark energy |
| 2.7 K | Temperature of the cosmic microwave background today |

## Do not mix these up

- **Expansion vs explosion:** an explosion throws material into space; expansion stretches space itself.
- **Dark matter vs dark energy:** dark matter pulls things together; dark energy speeds up the expansion.
- **Observable vs whole Universe:** we see only the part whose light has reached us.
- **Astronomy vs cosmology:** objects in the sky vs the Universe as a whole.

*Figures follow current estimates from the Planck mission, NASA and ESA. Reviewed 25 October 2026.*

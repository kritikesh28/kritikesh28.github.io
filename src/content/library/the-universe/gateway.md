Look up on a clear night and almost everything you see is older than the Earth. Some of that light left its star before humans existed.

This short course follows one question, **where did everything come from?**, in twelve small steps. Each page answers one question in about two minutes, then hands you the next one. You can stop after any page and still leave with a complete answer.

No maths is needed. Where a number matters, I explain where it comes from, and I tell you plainly when scientists do not know yet.

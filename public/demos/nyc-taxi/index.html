NYC Taxi Demand Explorer (2023)

Pick a weekday and an hour to see where yellow-taxi pickups happen across the city. Built from a 5% sample of NYC TLC 2023 trip records (about 1.8M cleaned trips), scaled up to an average day. Analysis: GitHub repo.

Numbers are estimates: sampled pickups divided by 0.05, then divided by the number of such weekdays in 2023. Colours use a square-root scale so mid-sized zones stay visible. About 1% of pickups have an unknown zone (TLC codes 264 and 265): they count in the total but can't be shown on the map. Pickups only, yellow taxis only. Zone shapes: NYC TLC taxi-zone shapefile, simplified. Built by Kritikesh Rajpurohit as part of an EPGP course project (IIIT Bangalore); designed with Claude.

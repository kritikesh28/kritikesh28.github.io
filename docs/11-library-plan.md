# 11 · Library plan

[Back to the handbook](README.md)

The blog is growing into an **open library for curious minds**: every subject broken into short, connected pages, from the most basic question up to expert depth. The full blueprint is a Claude Doc, "Curious Sparoows Library: blueprint". This page is the summary you can rely on without it.

## Page types for each unit (for example "Universe")

| Page | Length | Purpose | In Google? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Gateway** | 1 min | What you'll know by the end, number of parts, total time | Yes |
| **Spark** | 1 to 2 min | One question answered fully, a strong opening question, an honest hook to the next page | Yes |
| **Full Journey** | 10 to 25 min | All Sparks of the unit on one page, for reading at once | No (avoids duplicate content) |
| **Recap** | 1 min | The unit in 10 lines, a 5-question self-check, link to the next unit | Yes |

## Levels (proposed cosmic names; plain word always shown underneath)

Galaxy (course or subject) › Constellation (module) › Star (section) › Planet (chapter) › Moon (topic) › Spark (one page). Menus show at most three levels at once.

## Build phases

1. **Foundation:** the four page types, Next/Previous buttons, progress bar, search that never says "no match found", first 12 Universe Sparks.
2. **Explorer** (after the first unit): the Curiosity Orbit on the home page plus a plain directory, Hindi and Hinglish search words, tracking of empty searches.
3. **Grow** (after about 100 Sparks and a steady weekly rhythm): a map of links between subjects, an AI answer box that uses only the site's own pages, newsletter and quizzes, Hindi Sparks.

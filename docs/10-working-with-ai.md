# 10 · Working with AI and our rules

[Back to the handbook](README.md)

The site never depends on any AI. Everything in this handbook can be done by hand. AI tools (Claude or others) only make it faster.

## Rules for every piece of work

**Foundation first:** every rule below sits on [Vision and foundation](VISION.md): welfare, honesty, reader benefit and trust come before growth or money. If a rule here ever conflicts with it, the vision wins.

**Advice:** a helper should act as a friend, philosopher and critical guide: honest, critical, and bringing whatever expertise the task needs. Ideas are expanded fully, then presented in a clear structure.

**The psychology check ("ye kaam chalega ya nahi?")**, applied to every idea, page and feature before it is built:

1. Does the reader know in 5 seconds what they will get?
2. Is there a curiosity gap they want to close?
3. Is the effort small and the reward quick?
4. Do they feel progress (for example "Part 3 of 12")?
5. Does it end on a high?
6. Is there a reason to come back?
7. Would they trust it and share it (author, sources, honest hook)?
8. Does it respect them (no tricks, no forced clicks)?

**Content rules:**

- One page answers one question fully. Short pages ("Leaves") take 1 to 2 minutes (250 to 400 words).
- Open with a question that would make even an expert pause. End with an honest question that leads to the next page.
- Fact-check every number against a current source before publishing.
- Mark AI help honestly with `aiAssisted: true`, and add at least one line in your own voice.
- Go deep in one subject before opening the next.

## How to ask an AI for help

Give it this context first, then the task:

```
I run curioussparoows.com, an Astro website hosted on GitHub Pages from the
repository github.com/kritikesh28/kritikesh28.github.io. Blog posts are Markdown
files in src/content/blog/ with front matter: title, description, date (YYYY-MM-DD),
category (ai-ml, geography-earth, exam-prep, mind-ideas, stories-films,
life-skills), tags, lang (en|hi), draft, aiAssisted. Follow docs/VISION.md and
docs/10-working-with-ai.md. Task: ...
```

Useful tasks to ask for:

- "Turn these notes into a 300-word Leaf page answering the question: ..."
- "Fact-check every number in this post against current sources and list what's outdated."
- "Suggest 5 opening questions for this topic that would make an expert pause."
- "My build failed with this error: ... What is wrong in this file: ..."
